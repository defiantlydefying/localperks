# First-user observation guide

No first user or payment is documented yet. Use this guide with a willing creator or small business owner. Do not describe a guided fictional demo as a real paid collaboration.

1. Ask what they currently use to agree on deliverables, rights, and revisions. Ask for a past difficulty without collecting confidential contracts.
2. Open the public demo and ask them to choose their role without coaching. Note whether they find the role switch and primary action.
3. Have them try the scope-check sandbox with one correction, one additional deliverable, and one vague request. Ask what they think the verdict means and who makes the decision.
4. Ask them to draft a brief or check a caption using non-sensitive example text. Watch where labels or next steps confuse them.
5. Ask: “What would you use instead?”, “What would stop you using this?”, and “Would you pay for it today? If so, how much and for which workflow?” Record a refusal as faithfully as an enthusiastic answer. A suggested price is a hypothesis, not a sale.
6. Get permission before quoting or identifying them. Record role, date, exact or clearly paraphrased feedback, observed difficulty, willingness to pay, and an actual change prompted by the test.

The current product stores collaboration data in this browser. Role switching is a prototype; users on separate devices cannot transact together. Explain that limitation before asking about real adoption. Do not promise shared accounts, payments, or verified reach.
